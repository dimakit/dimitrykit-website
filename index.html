About

Who I am

I'm a product leader with 15+ years of experience working across payments infrastructure, embedded finance, and global product strategy. At Visa, I've helped build products that touch hundreds of millions of users, from BNPL pilots with Amazon to installment platforms spanning 30+ markets globally.

Before Visa, I spent time at Deloitte as a product manager and consultant, and before that built my foundation in finance and strategy at Mattel, Bank of America, and State Street. I have an MBA from UCLA Anderson.

The projects on this site aren't just hobbies. They are also how I stay sharp and on top of trends. I enjoy building things that solve problems I run into, testing hypotheses, and owning outcomes instead of handing it off to someone else.

Outside of work: I was born in the former USSR and went through two immigrations before landing on the East Coast of the United States. I'm raising a two-year-old and an Australian Shepherd, fitting in travel wherever I can, and I regularly enroll in courses and workshops to keep learning.
