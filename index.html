About

I'm a product leader with six years at Visa, working at the intersection of payments infrastructure, developer platforms, and product strategy. My job has always been to figure out what should be built — and make sure it actually ships.

The projects on this site aren't hobbies. They're how I stay sharp. I build things that solve problems I run into, test hypotheses I'm curious about, and prove — to myself and anyone paying attention — that I haven't outsourced execution to someone else.

Outside of work: I grew up between Ukraine and Israel, which gave me a natural comfort with ambiguity and a tendency to see problems from angles others miss. I'm raising a two-year-old and an Australian Shepherd, fitting in travel wherever I can, and always enrolled in some course on something I don't yet know.
